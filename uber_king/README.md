# Crusader Kings III - Uber King Mod

This mod adds several cheat traits and a powerful county modifier to Crusader Kings III, allowing you to become an "Uber King".

## Features

### Traits

*   **Otherworldly Stranger**: A powerful core trait that provides a wide range of bonuses, including increased income, health, and scheme power.
*   **Warfare Genius**: A military-focused trait that grants large bonuses to your armies, including increased levy size and reinforcement rate.
*   **Combat Genius**: A combat-focused trait that makes your character a formidable warrior, with bonuses to prowess, duel skill, and knight effectiveness.

### County Modifier

*   **County Combat Genius**: A powerful modifier that can be applied to any county, providing massive bonuses to the armies of its holder.

### Events

The mod includes events that allow you to add or remove the cheat traits and the county modifier at will.

## Installation

1.  Download the mod.
2.  Extract the files to your Crusader Kings III mod directory.
3.  Enable the mod in the Crusader Kings III launcher.

## Usage

Once the mod is enabled, you can use the following console commands to trigger the events:

*   `event cheats.1`: Opens the "Otherworldly Stranger" trait event.
*   `event cheats.2`: Opens the "Warfare Genius" trait event.
*   `event cheats.3`: Opens the "Combat Genius" trait event.
*   `event cheats.4`: Opens the "County Combat Genius" modifier event.
